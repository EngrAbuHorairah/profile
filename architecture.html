Infrastructure Architecture Showcase

A high-level view of how the five engineering domains in this portfolio relate to one another.

graph TD A[Enterprise Networking Fabric] --> B[HPC Compute Cluster] A --> C[AI Training & Inference Cluster] A --> D[EDA Compute Farm] B --> E[Parallel File System] C --> E D --> F[Distributed Storage - Ceph] E --> G[Backup & Disaster Recovery] F --> G B --> H[Monitoring & AIOps] C --> H D --> H A --> H

Networking (Project 10/11) underlies every compute domain. HPC and AI workloads (Projects 02, 04, 06) rely on the parallel file system (Project 13) for high-throughput I/O, while EDA (Projects 07-09) relies on the distributed storage platform (Project 12) and centralized licensing. Every domain feeds into unified monitoring/AIOps (Project 03/05) and is protected by the backup & DR platform (Project 14).
